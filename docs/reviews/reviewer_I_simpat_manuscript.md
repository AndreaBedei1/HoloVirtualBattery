# Reviewer I: hostile SIMPAT review of `paper/manuscript.md`

Scope: manuscript draft, read against `CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`, `FINAL_EXPERIMENTS.md`, `RELATED_WORK_VERIFICATION.md`, `FINAL_SIGNOFF.md`, `REVIEW_CONSOLIDATION.md`, `final_runs.csv`, `sensitivity.json`, `crosslayer.json`, `comm_matrix.json`, `energy_verification.json` (first run only), and `hostile_simpAT_review.md` (G-01..G-04). Nothing was edited except this file. `MODEL_EQUATIONS.md` was not read. `REVIEW_CONSOLIDATION.md` has no section 4 in the file as it stands (headings end at section 3); G-11 and G-16 are taken from section 1 and the section 3a list.

## Recommendation: REJECT in the present form (resubmission possible after the three changes below)

The manuscript is honest about its limits, which is to its credit. But honesty about limits is not a contribution. As written, a SIMPAT referee gets no validation, no comparator, no demonstrated decision that the method changes, and a headline output (grade shares) that leaves out the term that dominates the energy. Three of the four fatal findings of the earlier software review are relabelled, not answered (see "Status of G-01..G-04").

## Three changes that would most improve acceptability

1. Add an empirical anchor and one decision study. Compare predicted vs logged BlueROV2 pack current (or a bench discharge) for at least the thruster and battery chain, and show a scenario where grade shares/intervals change what a planner or designer would conclude relative to a baseline model (I-01, I-02).
2. Put hydrodynamics inside the method. Register drag and damping parameters with grades, report shares and intervals that include them, resolve the T200 grade (or show both), and replace the one-at-a-time offline checks with a closed-loop, multi-factor sensitivity analysis (I-03, I-11, I-12).
3. Rewrite as a V&V/methodology paper. State intended use and the validity argument, give the equations and grading algorithm in the paper, drop the layout, battery-fidelity and payload sections as findings, fix the comparator-free related work by reading S3 and S8 in full, and correct the labelling and unlisted claims (I-04 to I-08, I-10, I-14, I-05, I-06).
