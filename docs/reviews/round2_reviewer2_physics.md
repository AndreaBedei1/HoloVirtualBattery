## Verdict

Major revision of Section 12.3 only; the rest is sound or needs wording changes.

The drag/damping description (Eq. 17, 18), the attribution of the layout equality, zero still-water propulsion
and damping to the simulated body, and the Gabl/von Benzon range handling are correct and mostly well hedged.
The earlier-round critical items (reverse branch, B-01; transient windows, B-09; 1/(1+c dt) form, B-18) are
resolved in the final matrix (current is -x, forward branch; 30 s window; (1 - c dt) form).

The EXP10 part 5 inflow-bias derivation is **not consistent with its own stated assumption of propeller
similarity**. It applies a fixed-rpm "power per newton" ratio to a fixed-thrust operating point and omits the
rpm increase that fixed thrust requires at positive advance. The reported 7-9 % is therefore the lower end;
the same data and assumptions give about 12.5-15 %, and an independent actuator-disc check gives 14-17 %.
The sign (static tables under-predict) and the ranking conclusion at 0.5 m/s survive. The headline number,
Table 5 and the decision-case input do not.
