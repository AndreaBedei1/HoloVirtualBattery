## Verdict

**The battery and converter equations are implemented as the manuscript states them, and the evidence logic
(open sides, no silent upper bounds, unverified-conversion flag, supply-domain flag) is sound as far as it goes.
The decision case (Section 10) overreaches in three places and needs revision before it can be cited as
written: it silently drops two declared-unknown loads (R3-01), it lumps pack-direct loads into a 5 V converter
rail (R3-02), and its communication verdict ignores the conversion rule the paper defines (R3-03).** None of
these reverses a headline verdict (INDETERMINATE stays INDETERMINATE, the Gemini-below-Oculus ranking stays
unsupported), but the what-if leg counts (81-85, 88-91) and the "Supported" optical-vs-acoustic verdict are not
supported by the paper's own rules. Recommendation: minor-to-major revision of Section 10 and Table 3; no change
to the battery model needed.

## What I checked and found correct

- **Rint equations (Eq. 8-10).** `current_for_power` is the stable root `2P/(U+sqrt(U^2-4RP))`; loss `I^2 R`,
  chemical power `U I = P + I^2 R`, SOC update and heat `I^2 R - I T dU/dT` match the text.
  `current_limit` implements Eq. 10 including `U/(2R)` (stable branch) and `(U-Vcut)/R`.
- **Cutoff latch.** `step` latches only when no charge is left or `OCV <= Vcut`; between those states the pack is
  voltage-limited. Probe p4 (100-800 W constant power from SOC 1): terminal voltage never below 12.000 V,
  latch reason `voltage`, OCV work 266.8 Wh and energy balance `terminal + loss = OCV work` closes at every load
  (e.g. 800 W: 253.7 + 13.1 = 266.8 Wh). Time-step sensitivity: SOC error 0.07 % at dt = 1 s, 0.5 % at dt = 10 s
  after half a discharge; irrelevant at 100 Hz.
- **L0 bucket**, converter model (`P_in = sum P_rail / eta_rail`) and thruster voltage policy (`measured`
  refuses extrapolation; `table_voltage` is declared, reported and graded representative) are consistent with
  Section 3.3/4.3.
- **Upper bound vs typical.** Every upper-bound use in the decision configurations is labelled (`use: value_max`,
  `active_is_upper_bound`, `upper_bound_only` warnings). The decision JSON `upper_bound_energy_Wh` values
  (0.2928 / 0.3306 / 0.1889 Wh) reconcile with the per-device energies (camera 0.0236 + two lights 0.1417 + sonar).
  The D-grade review's statement that the maximum is never used silently as typical holds for the runs.
- **Rail verification and supply check mechanics.** `rail_verified` is False for payload and hotel in all three
  decision configurations (no efficiency record matches); the Gemini is flagged `supply_domain_mismatch`
  because its declared 19-74 V does not contain the bus range [12.0, 16.8] V; `unknown_contributions` then
  holds `imaging_sonar.supply_conversion`. This matches the JSON.
- **Arithmetic of the decision case.** Available energy 0.7 x 266.4 = 186.48 Wh; requirements 186.48/N; nominal
  maximum legs 88 / 86 / 92; what-if upper bounds `nominal + rail (1/eta - 1)`; Ping360-below-Oculus lower
  difference 0.0232 Wh = (10 - 5) W x 17 s minus a small propulsion term. All reproduce.
- **OCV grade D in claims.** The manuscript narrows C15/C16 correctly (model behaviour only, no endurance
  figure). Probe p4 confirms why: with OCV(0) = cutoff = 12.0 V the model delivers 100 % of rated charge at any
  load (OCV work 266.8 Wh at 100 W and at 800 W), so L1 cannot support any accessible-energy claim. See R3-04
  for the one place where the rated energy is nevertheless used as if usable.

## Specific answers to the questions asked

- **Battery equations, current limit, cutoff latch:** correct and consistent with Eq. 8-10 (probe p4).
- **Grade-D OCV in claims:** handled correctly in C15/C16; not handled in the decision case's leg counts (R3-04).
- **Does the decision case treat unverified conversion correctly?** Part A: yes for the Gemini step-up and the
  payload rail as a flag, but the rail is mis-scoped (R3-02) and two declared gaps are missing (R3-01). Part B:
  no (R3-03).
- **Is the 5 V rail lumped correctly?** No. It is one rail for loads on different supplies (R3-02).
- **Upper bound vs typical:** maxima are not used silently as typical in the runs; the inverse problem exists,
  derived "typical x tolerance" values are treated as closed bounds (R3-06).
- **Modem states, RX = LISTEN:** consistent with the registry note (no wake-up module, no separate listen mode;
  0.8 W is an upper bound; LISTEN interval [0, 0.8] W). Listening is 52 % of the acoustic nominal energy
  (61 s x 0.8 W), correctly described as a conditional point estimate in 9.6. The acoustic 24 V conversion is
  the gap in R3-03.
- **INDETERMINATE, rankings, what-if legs:** INDETERMINATE and the Gemini/Oculus non-ranking follow from the
  evidence. Ping360-below-both follows with the representative-family caveat (R3-09). The what-if leg counts do
  not follow under the paper's own rules (R3-01, R3-02, R3-04) and move by up to 4 legs.
- **Electrical claims beyond evidence:** the what-if sentence (81-85 / 88-91), Table 3 row 5 without a
  conversion condition, and the closed Navigator/Pi sides.
