# Lam, Chen, Bennett, Triantafyllou (OCEANS 2023 Limerick) - T200 propeller characterization

doi 10.1109/OCEANSLimerick52467.2023.10244513. 8 PDF pages. Page refs are PDF pages; figure/section numbers are the paper's. Read via pdftotext plus rendered pages 5-7 (Figs 10-17). Fig. 18 (p.8) was not rendered; per its caption it is the complete curve (KT, 10KQ, eta) for J 0-0.35 at 34.5 rev/s.

## 1. Facility and method
- Bollard: T200 on a stationary 6-DoF load cell in a large glass-walled tank (Sec. II-B, Figs 4, 7). Thrust = y-force. Each PWM level run 3 times (Fig. 10 caption).
- Dynamic: thruster on a 6-DoF load cell on a belt-driven tow carriage, tank ~8 m long, max carriage speed 1 m/s (Sec. II-C, Figs 8, 9). State 1 = carriage moving, thruster off (strut+thruster drag); state 2 = thruster on at fixed PWM; thrust = F_y(state 2) - F_y(state 1). Wall-interference check mentioned, no data.
- Measured: thrust (load cell); rpm (handheld laser tachometer, only in a separate bench PWM-to-rpm test, Figs 4/5; during towing rpm is NOT measured, taken from that map); battery voltage and current (voltage sensor + current sensor on the battery side, Arduino + data logger, logged every 300 ms: rev/s, A, V, W; Fig. 6); advance speed = carriage speed.
- Torque NOT measured: Q = Kt*I with Kt from a Blue Robotics velocity constant Kv (Sec. I-C.2, Q = I/(0.1047 Kv)); Kv value not stated.
- Not given: instrument makes/models/accuracy, calibration, uncertainty analysis, error bars (scatter only as repeated markers), water temperature/density, tank cross-section.

## 2. Hardware and conditions
- Propeller dia 3 in (D = 0.076 m); blade angle 22.5 deg at 0.75R (Table I, p.2); pitch distance 0.0704 m (text elsewhere 0.074), P/D = 0.97.
- Motor: T200 BLDC (no specs). ESC: unnamed (Blue Robotics PWM 1100-1900 us, 1500 neutral). Open loop, no speed feedback.
- Supply: nominally 14.8 V LiPo for all tests (Sec. II-A). Only one voltage; actual V per point not reported.
- Control: PWM 1500-1900 us tested (forward only), 40 us steps plus 1560 us ("15 %").
- Dynamic runs: (a) 1560 us = 11.6 rev/s (~696 rpm), J = 0.19-0.97 in 0.19 steps, ~3 repeats per point; (b) 1700 us = 34.5 rev/s (~2070 rpm), J ~0.06-0.31, 5 points. Conclusion says "32.6 rev/s" (inconsistent). Fig. 15 caption says "11.6 m/s" (typo).
- Bollard: ~400-3300 rpm.

## 3. Numeric results
- Fig. 5: PWM-to-rpm within 5 % of Blue Robotics data (BR 14 V and 16 V curves averaged as a 15 V proxy).
- Fig. 10 (p.5): bollard thrust vs rpm (x = rpm). Own fit T = 3.91e-6 x^2 - 0.0013 x - 0.44 (plot legend shows +0.000129x, inconsistent with caption); BR fit T = 4.40e-6 x^2 - 0.0011 x + 0.27. All points within 5 % of BR. Thrust ~0-50 N up to ~3400 rpm.
- Fig. 11 (p.6): measured electrical power vs rpm (caption), text mislabels it as vs advance ratio. Fit 1.36*exp(x/625) (poor). Read from figure, approx. (+-10 W): ~20 W at ~1000 rpm, ~55-70 W at 2000-2300 rpm, ~290 W at ~3300 rpm. Not compared with BR power table.
- Fig. 12: thrust/electrical power (N/W) vs rpm, scattered ~2-5.4 N/W, no fit.
- Fig. 13 (11.6 rev/s): thrust read from figure, approx.: ~1.0 N (J 0.19), 0.93 (0.38), 0.8 (0.57), 0.88 (0.76), 1.09 N (0.95); non-monotonic, authors cannot explain.
- Fig. 14 (11.6 rev/s): current read approx.: 0.235 A (J 0.19), 0.19 (0.38), 0.18 (0.57), 0.165 (0.76), 0.135 A (0.95).
- Fig. 15 (11.6 rev/s): KT and 10KQ vs J. Fits: 10KQ = -0.0679 J + 0.143; KT = -0.095 J + 0.238 (J<0.57), +0.165 J + 0.0832 (J>0.57). eta read ~0.4 at J 0.19 to ~4.7 at J 0.95 (quadratic fit); exceeds 1, flagged by authors as nonphysical.
- Fig. 16 (34.5 rev/s): thrust vs J, T = -12.8 J + 16.77 N; read ~16.2 N at J 0.06 to ~13 N at J 0.31.
- Fig. 17 (34.5 rev/s): current vs J, legend fit -1.091 J + 4.51 A, but markers read ~4.1 A at J 0.06 to ~3.7-3.8 A at J 0.31, so the stated intercept does not match the markers.
- Fig. 18 (34.5 rev/s): KT, 10KQ decreasing linearly, eta increasing linearly, J 0-0.35; values not read.
- Text: expected zero-thrust J from Fig. 16 line "2.3". Our arithmetic: 16.77/12.8 = 1.31, so the paper's value appears wrong.
- Derived by us (not in paper): KT ~0.42 at J~0, 34.5 rev/s (rho 1000, D 0.076); P ~ 4.1 A x 14.8 V ~ 60 W at 2070 rpm, consistent with Fig. 11.

## 4. Was electrical input power measured at different advance speeds?
Partly. Battery voltage and current were logged during towing (so V*I existed), and current vs J is plotted at fixed PWM (Figs 14, 17). Power vs J is never plotted, V per point is not tabulated, rpm is not measured while towing (open-loop ESC at fixed PWM can spin up as load falls, confounding J), torque/efficiency are derived (Q = Kt*I) and yield eta > 1. Only two command levels, one supply voltage, J <= 0.31 at 2070 rpm.

Inflow signal (read from figure, approx.): at 34.5 rev/s, between J 0.06 and 0.31 thrust falls ~20 % (16.2 -> 13 N) and current falls ~8-10 %.

## 5. Reverse thrust, BR comparison, limitations
- Reverse: not tested.
- BR comparison: thrust vs rpm and PWM vs rpm only (within 5 %); no power/efficiency comparison; 15 V interpolated from 14 and 16 V.
- Authors: carriage limited to 1 m/s; 11.6 rev/s run misbehaves (thrust rises J>0.55, eta up to ~5); suggest bigger tank and faster carriage; mid-range curve only to J=0.31.
- Ours: no uncertainty analysis; no temperature; strut-drag subtraction dominates error at ~1 N thrust; several internal inconsistencies listed above.

## 6. Assessment
Measured: bollard thrust vs rpm (3 reps); rpm vs PWM; bollard electrical power vs rpm at ~14.8 V; thrust and battery current vs J at two fixed PWM settings.
Derived (authors): torque, KQ, eta via Q = Kt*I (unreliable). Derived (us): KT ~0.42, P ~60 W cross-check.

Verdict: NOT sufficient for a validated P = f(T, V, Va). One supply voltage, two command levels, J <= 0.31 at the usable rpm, no measured rpm in tow, no power-vs-J, no uncertainties, no reverse.

Defensible optional approach: KT(J) linear decline (slope ~ -0.8 per unit J relative to KT0 at 2070 rpm, derived from Fig. 16: -12.8/16.77) combined with motor+ESC efficiency taken from the static BR tables (P_elec = Q*omega/eta_m, eta_m function of load/rpm/V only, not J). That assumption is plausible but untested here; KQ must come from another source since this paper's KQ uses an unstated Kt. Label as weak evidence, default off, with large uncertainty (>=10-20 % suggested, our judgement). Qualitative trend usable: at fixed command, inflow reduces thrust more than it reduces current, so power per newton rises with Va relative to bollard.
