# Tun, Huang, Preece (2026) - EO-LQT for UUV fish net-pen visual inspection (IJRR, doi 10.1177/02783649261453998)

Source: pdftotext of the 30-page PDF (raw text kept alongside as tun_raw.txt). Page numbers are journal pages 1-30. Table 4 text extraction is garbled; row/column mapping below was cross-checked against the Section 8.2.3 and Section 10 prose.

## Verdict (strict)
- (a) Independent electrical energy validation: NO.
- (b) External consistency check of vehicle motion / controller energy: only WEAK, qualitative at best (relative controller ranking, motion model, Table 8 parameters). Not a quantitative cross-check of absolute Wh.
- (c) Effectively "neither" for electrical validation; usable only as a literature source for a BlueROV2 Heavy parameter set and for the T200 power-polynomial approach.

## Key finding on energy measurement
The paper never mentions a battery, battery voltage/current, power module, current sensor, ESC telemetry or any electrical logging (text search for battery/voltage/power module/ESC found nothing relevant). Energy Et (Wh) is computed from the controller's commanded thrust through a T200 thrust-to-power polynomial.
- Sec. 4.2, eqs (29)-(38), pp. 9-10: thruster mechanical power model; eq (33) linear approx p = c_p * f * ... (c_p = 0.022, R2 = 0.96, Fig. 7, p10); eq (37) 6th-order polynomial p_T,j(f_T,j), coefficients c0..c6 listed under Fig. 8 (p10), R2 = 0.99, built from the "publicly available dataset of T200 thruster, operating at 16 V" (Blue Robotics web page, accessed 3 Nov 2023). Eq (38): Et = sum_j p_T,j * dt per control loop (p10).
- p4 (contributions list): use of the T200 thruster power function (6th-order polynomial) "to compare real-time energy consumption among controllers in both high-fidelity simulations and pool experiments." Abstract (p1) likewise names the "T200 thruster power function" as the metric.
- Therefore, in the pool experiments the Wh values are model-reconstructed from commanded thrust (controller output after saturation, eq 52/62), not measured. Thruster angular velocity is also inferred from f_T (eq 34), so no actual RPM/ESC data. Hotel load (computer, lights, camera) is not included; the controller runs on a topside computer via tether (p25).
- The 16 V dataset is a static bench curve (steady thrust vs power): no dynamic/transient effects, no battery voltage sag.

- Thrusters: m = 8 T200 (4 horizontal vectored at 45 deg, 4 vertical) (Table 8, p29). Layout per Fig. 3 (p4).

Onboard payload/camera of the ROV not specified. ZED 2i camera is external (Sec 7.1, p15; Figs 11-12, pp. 15-16).

## Controllers (Secs 3-6, pp. 8-13; Table 1, p12)
Baseline CO-LQT1 (constant Q, R) vs five EO-LQT variants: EO-LQT1 (kinetic-energy Lagrangian in Q), EO-LQT2 (quadratic power in R), EO-LQT3 (R plus non-quadratic power k_p^T f_T), EO-LQT4 (2+3), EO-LQT5 (1+3). Finite-horizon LQT with backward integration of S, s, s0 each loop (HJB, Sec 6). Gains/tuning: Appendix A.2 (pp. 28-29); 15 Hz loop, Nh = 3 (sim) / 5 (pool) steps, t_ahead = 0.067 s; Table 9 (p30).

## Energy definition
Sum over thrusters of the thrust-based mechanical power via 6th-order polynomial, integrated over time (eqs 37-38, p10). Kinetic energy Ek = 1/2 nu^T M nu (eq 28, p9) is used inside the cost function only, not as the reported metric. Potential energy neglected (Remark 4.1, p9). Reported metric is Et in Wh; Et ratio = (E1 - Ej)/E1 (eq 79, p17).

## Missions / environment
- Motivating site: NZKS Blue Endeavour (offshore, Cook Strait); square pen 30 x 30 x 15 m, manual inspection about 2.5 h (p2, Fig 1); site specs Table 6 (p28: D 53.5 m, h 15 m, currents 0.5-0.9 m/s), Fig 19 (p28). No field trial was conducted (p14: budget, localization, logistics; p25 future work).
- FNVI simulation (Gazebo / UUV Simulator, ROS Melodic, Ubuntu 18.04; Table 5, p28): 3D complicated trajectory around the pen (Fig 10, p14), 6 controllers x 3 constant uniform irrotational currents (0, 0.5, 0.9 m/s along +y; Table 7, p28) = 18 runs (Table 2, p14). Fig 13 (p18) trajectories, Fig 14 (pp. 20-21) pose errors, Fig 15 (p22) energy.
- Pool experiments on the real BlueROV2 Heavy plus matched pool simulation: planar 2D trajectory (curvature, straight lines, sudden twist change), no current (p14), 6 controllers x 1 run each (Table 2: 30 runs total, so n = 1 per condition, no repeats or statistics). Pool dimensions, trajectory speeds and durations are NOT stated. Localization: ZED 2i + 2 AprilTags + Kalman filter (Sec 7.1, pp. 15-17); start waypoints varied between runs (Fig 16, p22).

## Assessment for HoloEnergy
- (a) Independent electrical validation: no. Energy is model-reconstructed from commanded thrust through a static 16 V T200 curve in both simulation and pool, so it shares the same thrust-to-power assumption any T200-curve-based simulator uses. There is no measured electrical power, no battery data, no ESC/power-module logging, and no hotel load.
- (b) External consistency: weak. Potentially useful for (i) an order-of-magnitude plausibility check that a thrust-to-power polynomial gives about 1 Wh for a short planar BlueROV2 Heavy pool trajectory (durations not stated, so not scalable), (ii) the qualitative effect that thrust-penalising controllers lower Et at the cost of tracking error, and (iii) a published BlueROV2 Heavy parameter set (Table 8) to compare against. Limits: surface-proximate operation with tether and fixture, n = 1 runs, external camera localization, unidentified damping source, no durations/speeds, no current in pool tests.
- Recommended use: cite as a literature example of T200-curve-based energy accounting and of BlueROV2 Heavy dynamics parameters (flag provenance as unverified), not as ground truth.
